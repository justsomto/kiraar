ABOUT KIRAAR

100,000+ Users Later.

Our ultimate goal is to build the most expansive, trusted service network starting right here in Africa and eventually expanding across global borders. We believe that access to reliable home and professional services shouldn't be a luxury or a gamble.

By seamlessly matching highly skilled, vetted local experts with active clients in their immediate communities, kiraar is doing more than just fixing everyday problems—we are architecting a sustainable economic ecosystem. We empower independent artisans, scale their business visibility, and provide secure, cross-border economic opportunities that uplift local economies from the ground up.

Problem finding reliable handymen?

We know how it feels

It all started on a cold night, an unfortunate mishap, and a solution-driven founder. You see, kiraar was born out of a need. A need that was created when our founder and his family got stuck in the middle of the night in southern Nigeria.

Their car broke down at the worst time possible, and there was no easy way to find a trusted artisan. The journey to getting their vehicle fixed felt like a never-ending horror movie. Our founder knew right then that no one else should ever have to go through that. A few months later, kiraar was created—engineered to ensure no one ever gets stranded or stressed by sudden service needs again.

Find artisans with ease!

Emergencies happen when you least expect them, and there is a big chance you have struggled to find capable, qualified, and reliable professionals at the exact point of need. Even when you do find someone, there is rarely any assurance of their skill, capability, or character.

What if your car breaks down in the middle of nowhere?

What if your plumbing fails unexpectedly?

What if you could access competent, certified, and vetted artisans right around you with just a single click? No more what-ifs. With kiraar, you can instantly search for verified professionals within your immediate surroundings and hire them in less than 5 minutes. This platform works for you anytime, anywhere across Nigeria.

What we offer

Our platform empowers you to research, hire, rate, and review local home service handymen—including expert carpenters, electricians, painters, plumbers, and more—all from one seamless dashboard.

Beyond daily repairs, kiraar offers smart tools to get accurate cost estimates for major renovation projects, alongside a rich library of "how-to" resources to help you understand common domestic fixes.

In an emerging market like Africa, kiraar is fundamentally transforming how people connect with local talent and manage their homes more effectively and efficiently. Our absolute goal is to simplify local logistics and create a completely hassle-free, stress-free process for our users. At kiraar, our dream is simple: when you need help, you get help instantly.

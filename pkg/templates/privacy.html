Data Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

Your data privacy and identity security are paramount to us.

NDPA 2023 & NDPR Compliance

Kiraar Technologies ("Kiraar", "we", "us", or "our") operates strictly under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, and safeguard your personal and financial information when you access or use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To connect reliable artisans with homeowners and businesses in Nigeria and facilitate secure escrow transactions, we collect the following categories of data:

Category Data Types Collected Applicability
Identity Data Full legal name, email address, phone number, physical address/LGA, profile photos, and state of residence. All Users
Artisan Verification Data National Identification Number (NIN), government ID, trade credentials, certifications, guarantor information, and criminal background checks. Artisans & Professionals Only
Financial & Payout Data Bank name, Nigerian account number, account name, and secure payout transfer codes. (Card details are tokenized under strict PCI-DSS standards by certified banking infrastructure - Kiraar never stores sensitive card numbers or CVV). Clients (Payment tokens) • Professionals (Bank payout info)
Communications & Audio In-app chat transcripts, images of repairs/jobs, file attachments, and recorded voice notes exchanged between clients and artisans. All Users
Technical & Log Data IP address, browser type, operating system, and session timestamps. All Users

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate business and contractual purposes, including:

  • Service Connection: Allowing clients to search, discover, book, and communicate directly with vetted local artisans.
  • Escrow & Bank Transfers: Safeguarding booking funds in secure escrow and releasing 90% payouts to artisans upon verified job completion.
  • Identity & Safety Checks: Verifying the credentials of artisans to protect home and commercial property owners across Nigeria.
  • Dispute Resolution: Reviewing booking history and communication logs when handling customer complaints or refund requests.
  • Platform Security: Preventing fraud, multi-accounting abuse, and unsolicited spam.

3. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Processors

Kiraar integrates with trusted enterprise infrastructure to deliver reliable services. These third parties are bound by strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements:

Licensed Payment Infrastructure

CBN-licensed banking infrastructure for processing debit cards, bank transfers, escrow holding, and automated artisan payouts.

CometChat

Real-time encrypted text, voice note, and media messaging infrastructure connecting clients and artisans.

Identity Partners

NIMC-authorized verification partners for validating National Identity Numbers (NIN) of registered artisans.

4. Data Retention & Security Measures

We retain personal data for as long as your account remains active or as required by Nigerian financial regulatory and tax retention laws (generally 6 years for financial transaction records).

Our security measures include 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit, AES-256 encrypted database backups, restricted internal role-based access controls, and continuous vulnerability monitoring.

5. Your Rights Under the NDPA

As a data subject in Nigeria, you have guaranteed statutory rights:

Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information Kiraar holds about you.
Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate personal or professional details at any time.
Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request account deletion and removal of your personal profile.
Right to Object: Object to the processing of your data for marketing or promotional campaigns.

6. Cookies & Session Management

Kiraar uses essential HTTP cookies and secure session tokens to keep you logged in, protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF), and maintain your active cart/booking sessions. We do not use third-party invasive tracking cookies without your consent.

7. Policy Updates

Kiraar reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy as our services evolve or to reflect changes in Nigerian law. Any significant modifications will be communicated via email or posted prominently on our website.

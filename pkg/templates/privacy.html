Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: September 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Kiraar Technologies ("Kiraar", "we", "us", or "our"), safeguarding your personal data is a foundational responsibility. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), overseen by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

1. Data Controller Information

Kiraar Technologies operates as the Data Controller responsible for your personal data when you interact with our platform, website, or services in Nigeria.

For any inquiries regarding data protection, access requests, or compliance, our designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) can be contacted at: franklinsomto7@gmail.com.

2. Lawful Basis for Processing Personal Data

Under Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we only collect and process personal data under the following lawful bases:

Contract Performance

Processing needed to create your account, connect you with artisans, manage bookings, and execute escrow payouts.

Explicit Consent

Freely given, informed consent when registering, uploading verification documents, or opting into communications.

Legal Obligation

Compliance with Nigerian financial, consumer protection (FCCPA), and anti-money laundering regulations.

Legitimate Interests

Fraud detection, identity verification, on-site safety, dispute investigation, and cybersecurity defense.

3. The Categories of Personal Data We Collect

Depending on whether you use Kiraar as a Client or an Artisan / Professional, we collect the following data:

Data Category Specific Elements Collected Applicable User
Identity & Profile Data First name, last name, business trade name, profile photo, bio, and service specialization. Clients & Professionals
Contact Information Email address, active Nigerian telephone number, and physical service/delivery address. Clients & Professionals

Technical Telemetry IP address, browser profile, device operating system, login timestamps, and session cookies. All Visitors

4. How We Utilize Your Information

  • Service Connection: Allowing clients to search, discover, book, and communicate directly with vetted local artisans.
  • Escrow & Bank Transfers: Safeguarding booking funds in escrow via Paystack and releasing 90% payouts to artisans upon completion.
  • Identity & Safety Checks: Verifying the credentials of artisans to protect home and commercial property owners across Nigeria.
  • Dispute Resolution: Reviewing booking history and communication logs when handling customer complaints or refund requests.
  • Transactional Alerts: Delivering booking confirmations, price adjustment notices, payment receipts, and payout alerts via email and SMS.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing multiple unauthorized accounts, detecting off-platform payment schemes, and protecting platform integrity.

5. Sharing with Third-Party Service Processors

Kiraar does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers. We share data only with vetted service providers operating under strict data processing agreements:

Paystack Payments

CBN-licensed payment gateway for processing debit cards, bank transfers, escrow holding, and automated artisan payouts.

CometChat Infrastructure

Provides real-time, end-to-end secured chat infrastructure between clients and artisans per booking.

Law Enforcement & Regulators

Shared only when strictly mandated by Nigerian court orders, the NDPC, FCCPC, or statutory security agencies.

6. Your Data Rights Under the NDPA 2023

As a data subject in Nigeria, you are entitled to comprehensive rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act:

Right to Access

Request confirmation and a digital copy of all personal records Kiraar holds about you.

Right to Rectification

Update, amend, or correct inaccurate or incomplete contact, business, or bank account details.

Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten")

Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records, subject to statutory tax and financial retention laws.

Right to Object & Restrict

Object to or restrict specific processing operations, including marketing and algorithmic matching.

To exercise any of these rights, email our Data Protection Desk at franklinsomto7@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days as required by Nigerian law.

7. Data Storage, Retention & Security

We deploy robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation:

  • Security Controls: TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit, bcrypt-hashed passwords, CSRF session protection, and role-based administrative access limits.
  • Retention Timelines: Active account data is retained for the lifespan of your account. Transaction and payout logs are retained for 6 years to comply with Nigerian financial, tax, and audit regulations.
  • Breach Notification: In the unlikely event of a data breach posing risk to your rights, Kiraar will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours and inform impacted users promptly.

8. Protection of Minors

Kiraar is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account on Kiraar, please contact us immediately for prompt investigation and account deletion.

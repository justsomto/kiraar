We have answers to your Questions!

kiraar is a local service platform that connects homeowners and businesses with verified, registered domestic and professional service providers right in their neighborhood. You simply open the platform, search for the service you need, select a trusted provider closest to you, book a time, and get the job done.

Yes, absolutely. Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities. Every service provider listed on kiraar must undergo a strict onboarding process, which includes identity verification and the submission of official registration documents before they can accept jobs.

Booking services through kiraar is free for customers. You only pay for the actual service delivered by the provider. Prices are transparent, and you can discuss the scope and final cost directly with your chosen provider before work begins.

Getting started is easy!

Register on the platform as a provider.

Complete your business profile by selecting your trade or specialized skill.

Submit the required identification and verification documents. Once our team reviews and approves your application, you’ll be listed locally and ready to receive requests.

Once your profile is active, kiraar lists you under your specific trade based on your location. When nearby users search for your services, your profile appears. If they choose you, you’ll receive an instant service request that you can accept or decline based on your availability.

All payments are handled securely through the platform. Once you accept a request, complete the job on-site, and the customer confirms successful delivery, your earnings are processed and deposited securely into your linked account.

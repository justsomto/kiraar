Governing access to and use of the Kiraar artisan marketplace in Nigeria.

Important Legal Notice

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before creating an account, booking an artisan, or offering professional services on Kiraar. By registering or using any part of this platform, you agree to be bound by these Terms and our Escrow & Refund Policy.

1. Definitions & Platform Role

"Kiraar" (referred to as "we", "us", or "our") is an online technology marketplace operating in Nigeria that connects consumers ("Clients") seeking home, repair, and trade services with vetted independent contractors, handymen, and artisans ("Professionals" or "Artisans").

Independent Contractor Relationship: Artisans registered on Kiraar are independent service providers, not employees, agents, or partners of Kiraar Technologies. Kiraar provides matching technology, scheduling tools, encrypted messaging, and escrow transaction settlement.

2. User Eligibility & Account Registration

  • Age: You must be at least 18 years of age to register an account on Kiraar.
  • Dual Accounts: Kiraar allows a single Nigerian phone number and email address to hold both a Client Profile and a Professional Profile, accessible via our role switcher without needing multiple accounts.
  • Artisan Vetting: All professionals must undergo identity verification via National Identification Number (NIN) checks and skill vetting before their profiles are made active to receive bookings.
  • Account Security: You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials. You must promptly notify Kiraar if you suspect unauthorized access.

3. Booking and Service Appointments

When a Client books an Artisan through Kiraar, the two parties enter into a direct agreement regarding the scope, scheduled date, and price of the work.

  • In-App Negotiation: Price negotiations and scheduling must be conducted via Kiraar's in-app chat or booking portal so a clear record exists in case of disputes.
  • Price Adjustments: Either party may propose a revised booking price before payment is completed. Once a payment is funded into escrow, price changes require mutual written consent via the platform.
  • Access to Premises: Clients must provide safe, lawful, and reasonable access to the property where services are to be performed.

4. Prohibition of Off-Platform Payments

Strict Policy: All payments for services arranged on Kiraar must be processed through Kiraar's escrow payment gateway. Direct cash or private bank transfers are strictly prohibited.

Artisans soliciting direct cash payments and Clients offering off-platform compensation face immediate account suspension, forfeiture of escrow protection, and loss of verified status. Direct payments void all refund eligibility and insurance backing.

5. Escrow Payment System & Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Compliance

All financial transactions on Kiraar are processed in Nigerian Naira (₦) through licensed, Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)-regulated banking and payment infrastructure partners.

Escrow Protection

When a client pays, the money does not go directly to the artisan. It is safely held in an encrypted escrow account until the service is completed.

48-Hour Auto-Release Rule

Clients are requested to release payment upon satisfactory job delivery. If no dispute or complaint is raised within 48 hours (2 days) following completion, funds auto-release to the artisan.

Please review our full Escrow & Refund Policy for dispute procedures, payout timelines, and refund criteria.

6. Fees & Commission

  • Platform Commission: Kiraar retains a 10% facilitation fee from each completed booking price.
  • Artisan Payout (90%): The artisan receives 90% of the agreed booking price paid directly into their verified Nigerian commercial bank account via automated bank transfer.
  • Taxes: Users are responsible for complying with applicable personal or corporate tax obligations under the Nigeria Finance Act and Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) guidelines.

7. User Conduct & Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Submit false, misleading, fraudulent, or counterfeit identification credentials.

  • Post defamatory, discriminatory, or offensive language in reviews, portfolio items, or public bio descriptions.
  • Attempt to bypass security features, reverse engineer the platform, or execute unauthorized automated scraping.

8. Dispute Resolution & Complaints

If a dispute arises concerning service delivery, workmanship, property damage, or non-completion:

  1. The Client must file a formal complaint through the Kiraar Bookings dashboard within 48 hours of completion.
  2. Kiraar's operations team freezes escrow payout while reviewing chat logs, photo evidence, and scheduled scopes.
  3. If the parties cannot reach mutual agreement, Kiraar will render a final binding resolution, which may include a full refund, partial refund, or order for remediation.

To the maximum extent permitted by Nigerian law, Kiraar Technologies shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages resulting from the actions, omissions, or workmanship of independent Artisans hired through the platform, except to the extent of funds held under our escrow guarantee.

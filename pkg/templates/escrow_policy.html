Escrow & Refund Policy

Guaranteed safety for clients • Guaranteed payment for artisans

How Kiraar Escrow Protects You

In Nigeria, one of the biggest risks when hiring local artisans is paying upfront and getting substandard work — or for artisans, finishing hard work and struggling to get paid. Kiraar solves this through our secure Escrow Protection system. Client money is never handed directly to the artisan until the job is satisfactorily delivered.

The 4-Step Escrow Process

1
Book & Agree

Client and artisan agree on price and scope of work via the in-app chat.

2
Pay into Escrow

Client pays securely online. Funds are held safely in escrow.

3
Work Delivered

The vetted artisan visits your premises and completes the scheduled job.

4
Release & Payout

Client confirms job, and the artisan receives 90% payout in their Nigerian bank account.

1. The 48-Hour (2-Day) Auto-Release Rule

To ensure hardworking Nigerian artisans are not kept waiting indefinitely for their earned income:

  • Manual Release: Clients are encouraged to click "Release Payment" on their bookings page immediately once the artisan finishes and the work is verified.
  • Automated Release: If a job is marked completed and the client neither releases payment nor files a complaint within 48 hours (2 days), Kiraar's automated escrow scheduler automatically releases the payout to the artisan's registered bank account.
  • Grace Period: Clients receive email notifications alerting them that their booking was marked completed, giving them ample time to inspect the work and raise any concerns.

2. Platform Fee & Artisan Payout Structure

Kiraar maintains an open and transparent fee model without hidden deductions:

90%

Artisan Payout

Sent straight to the artisan's verified Nigerian commercial bank account (GTBank, Zenith, Access, First Bank, Kuda, etc.) via automated secure bank transfer.

10%

Platform Facilitation Fee

Powers artisan identity verification (NIN checks), banking transaction fees, 24/7 customer support, and secure hosting infrastructure.

3. Refund Eligibility & Guidelines

Clients are fully protected and entitled to refunds under the following verified circumstances:

Scenario Refund Type Timeline
Artisan No-Show 100% Full Refund Processed within 24 hours of confirmation
Cancellation Before Work Commences 100% Full Refund Instant reversal to original payment source
Incomplete or Substandard Work Partial or Full Refund Subject to dispute review within 48 hours
Mutual Cancellation on On-Site Scope Mismatch 100% Full Refund Processed immediately upon mutual agreement

4. How to Dispute a Job Before Funds Are Released

If you are not satisfied with an artisan's service delivery:

  1. Act Promptly: File your complaint from your Client Bookings page within 48 hours of the job being marked completed.
  2. Immediate Freeze: Filing a complaint automatically halts all payout transfers, preventing funds from leaving escrow.
  3. Provide Evidence: Submit clear photos or descriptions of the issue in the complaint portal.
  4. Resolution: Our operations desk investigates within 24 to 48 hours to either arrange for the artisan to rectify the issue or issue your refund.

Warning Against Direct Cash Payments

If an artisan asks you to pay them directly in cash, via their personal account number, or outside the Kiraar platform, do not agree. Direct off-platform payments immediately void all Kiraar escrow protections, customer guarantees, and refund eligibility. Always report any provider soliciting off-platform cash to our support team.
